The Plant care bot

The Green School's Plant care bot is a smart robot that automatically takes care of your plants, which helps schools to make their environment greener and more eco-friendly more easily. Thanks to its smart sensors, the bot can keep an eye on the plant's status and automatically water it when necessary. That way the plants can stay healthy without students or teachers having to spend a lot of time on them.

User manual

Place de plant in the pots or planters that belongs to the system.

  • Place the product on a stable location in the classroom or another room.
  • Fill the water reservoir with clean water to the specified maximum level.
  • Check if the the water hose or the watering system is connected correctly.
  • Check if the sensors are placed in the correct places.
  • Turn the product on and check if the system works correctly.

After turning on the system, it will automatically measure the following parameters around the plant.

  • Lightsensor: measures how much light the plant receives.
  • Humidity meter: measures how much humidity is in the soil.
  • Temperature sensor: measures the temperature of the environment around the plant.
  • Automatic watering system: when the soil gets too dry, the system automatically gives water.
  • Water level sensor: measures how much water is left in the water tank.

If needed change the desired thresholds for the humidity of the soil and the water level. The correct settings may differ per plant species.

How does the product work?

When the product is turned on, the sensors measure the environment around the plant. The humidity sensor measures the amount of water in the soil.

If the soil is too dry, the system will activate the waterpump. The pump will pump water from the reservoir to the plant.

The Lightsensor measures the amount of light the plant receives and the temperature sensor measures the temperature of the environment. With the data from these sensors, the system can determine the status of the plant.

When the water tank is almost empty, the system will send an alarm to the user. They'll know when to refill the tank.

Steps for refilling the water tank:

  • Checks if the alarm goes off for a low water level.
  • Open the water tank according to the instructions of the product.
  • Fill the reservoir with clean water to the specified maximum level.
  • Close the tank as much as possible.
  • Check if the alarm stops and the water level is correctly displayed.

Don't fill the tank above the maximum level. Check regularly if there is enough water available.

To make the product work properly, it is important to check the system regularly:

  • Check if the tank has enough water.
  • Check if the sensors are placed in the correct places.
  • Clean the sensors and the tank regulary according to the maintenance instructions.
  • Check if de waterpump works when the soil is too dry.
  • Check if the alarm works when the water level gets too low.
  • Check if the plant has enough light and is in a suitable temperature.
